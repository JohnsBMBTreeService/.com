Signs Your Trees Need Trimming

  • Branches touching or overhanging the roof: Causes shingle wear, moisture buildup, and easy access for pests.
  • Low-hanging limbs over yards or driveways: Interferes with mowing, vehicles, or safe walking clearance.
  • Dead or crossing branches inside the crown: Weakens the canopy and creates sudden drop hazards in high wind.
  • Dense, overgrown canopy before hurricane season: Heavy outer weight increases limb failure during tropical storms.

Trimming vs. Removal — Honest On-Site Advice

Tree Trimming FAQs

Do you top trees?

No. Topping weakens trees, causes decay, and creates fast-growing weak shoots that snap easily in storms. We use proper structural pruning and crown thinning instead.

When is the best time to trim trees on the Mississippi Gulf Coast?

Deadwood and roof-clearance pruning can be done year-round, while major structural pruning on oaks is often best in late fall through winter—or well ahead of peak hurricane season.

Do you haul away all the trimmed branches?

Yes, complete brush removal and yard cleanup are included unless you ask us to leave wood chips or firewood rounds behind.
