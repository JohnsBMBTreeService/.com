Warning Signs a Tree Should Be Evaluated

451
    452
  • Sudden or worsening lean: Soil heaving, lifted roots, or a new tilt after heavy Gulf Coast rain.
    • 453
  • Deadwood & falling limbs: Large bare branches hanging over roofs, driveways, or play areas.
    • 454
  • Trunk cracks, hollows, or fungal growth: Deep splits, soft wood, or mushrooms growing around the root flare.
    • 455
  • Storm or lightning damage: Split leaders or stripped bark that compromises the tree's strength before hurricane season.
