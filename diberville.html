In newer builds and up Lamey Bridge Road, pines left standing after lot clearing can be stressed by changed grade and drainage, and Back Bay-side lots deal with wet ground and wind off the water.

Newer D'Iberville subdivisions often have HOA covenants and utility easements that affect which trees can come down and where equipment can go, while older lots tend to be tight, with fences and close setbacks. Lots beside drainage ditches or retention ponds need careful debris routing.
