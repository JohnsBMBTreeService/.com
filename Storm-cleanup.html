What to Do Right After a Storm

  • Check for hazards from a safe distance: Look up for split limbs and look down for hidden wires or wet branches.
  • Take clear photos before moving debris: Helpful both for accurate quotes and if you are documenting property damage for insurance.
  • Avoid DIY chainsaw work on tangled or suspended trunks: Storm-loaded timber can spring or roll violently when cut.
  • Text or call us with your town and photos: Sending a quick photo helps us bring the right rigging and crew setup on the first trip.
